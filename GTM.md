# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target User

The primary target user is:
- startup founders
- engineering managers
- AI-heavy product teams
- SaaS startups with 5–100 employees

These users actively spend money on AI tools such as:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Cursor
- GitHub Copilot
- Gemini

Most of these teams lack visibility into overlapping subscriptions and unnecessary AI spending.

---

# User Trigger Moments

Users are likely to search for this tool when:
- monthly SaaS bills increase
- multiple AI tools are purchased
- finance teams request cost reduction
- startups begin scaling engineering teams
- founders review operational expenses

Typical searches may include:
- “reduce AI subscription costs”
- “best ChatGPT alternatives”
- “AI tool cost optimization”
- “startup SaaS cost reduction”

---

# Online Communities

Target users commonly spend time in:
- Reddit communities such as r/startups and r/SaaS
- Indie Hackers
- Product Hunt
- Hacker News
- X (Twitter) startup communities
- AI engineering Discord servers
- LinkedIn startup founder groups

---

# First 100 Users Strategy

The first 100 users can be acquired through:
- posting demo videos on LinkedIn
- sharing screenshots on X
- launching on Product Hunt
- posting in startup subreddits
- sharing in college founder communities
- direct outreach to startup founders

The application is naturally shareable because users can compare savings opportunities.

---

# Organic Growth Loop

The viral loop works through:
- screenshot sharing
- AI audit comparisons
- founder referrals
- public discussion around AI overspending

Users discovering significant savings are likely to share results with peers.

---

# Unfair Distribution Advantage

Credex has a strong advantage because:
- the company already operates in the AI infrastructure space
- the platform directly solves a real financial pain point
- the audit naturally funnels users toward discounted AI credits

This creates a highly aligned lead-generation model.

---

# Early Success Metrics

Week 1 traction would ideally include:
- 100+ completed audits
- 25+ saved reports
- 10+ consultation leads
- 5+ organic social shares

These metrics would validate product-market interest.

---

# Long-Term Expansion

Future growth opportunities include:
- enterprise AI budgeting tools
- benchmarking dashboards
- organization-wide spend monitoring
- AI procurement analytics
- subscription management automation

The long-term vision is to become a “Mint for AI spending.”

# Product Metrics

## North Star Metric

### Completed AI Audits Per Week

The primary success metric for the platform is the number of completed AI audits per week.

This metric directly measures:
- user engagement
- product usefulness
- lead-generation effectiveness
- growth potential

A completed audit indicates that a user received meaningful value from the platform.

---

# Input Metrics

## 1. Audit Completion Rate

Measures:
- percentage of visitors who complete the audit flow

This helps evaluate:
- onboarding quality
- form usability
- landing page effectiveness

---

## 2. Report Save Rate

Measures:
- percentage of users who save their audit report

This indicates:
- perceived audit value
- lead quality
- user trust

---

## 3. Consultation Request Rate

Measures:
- percentage of high-savings users requesting additional consultation

This directly impacts:
- conversion opportunities
- business revenue potential

---

# Metrics To Instrument First

The first analytics events that should be tracked include:

- audit_started
- audit_completed
- report_saved
- download_clicked
- consultation_requested
- AI_summary_generated

Tracking these events provides visibility into:
- user behavior
- drop-off points
- feature adoption
- conversion performance

---

# Pivot Indicators

Potential pivot indicators include:

- low audit completion rate
- weak report save rates
- low engagement after audit generation
- minimal consultation requests

For example:
- if fewer than 10% of users complete audits consistently,
  the onboarding experience may require redesign.

---

# Long-Term Product Metrics

Future product metrics could include:
- average savings identified per company
- average AI spend per team
- repeat audit usage
- benchmark participation
- referral conversion rates

---

# Why These Metrics Matter

The platform is fundamentally a:
- lead-generation tool
- AI cost optimization platform
- SaaS intelligence product

Therefore, metrics should focus on:
- audit value delivery
- lead quality
- conversion efficiency
- user engagement

instead of traditional social metrics such as daily active users.

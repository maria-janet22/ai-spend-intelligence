# Assumptions

## 1. Monthly Billing

All calculations assume monthly subscription pricing.

---

## 2. Per User Pricing

Most AI tools follow per-user pricing models.

Example:
- 10 users × $20 = $200/month

---

## 3. Savings Opportunities

Savings are calculated based on:
- overlapping tools
- duplicate subscriptions
- unnecessary premium plans
- underutilized accounts

---

## 4. Optimization Logic

The system recommends:
- reducing redundant subscriptions
- consolidating tools
- switching to lower-cost alternatives

---

# Example Scenario

## Input

- ChatGPT Plus
- Claude Pro
- 10 team members
- $400/month total spend

## Output

- estimated savings: $120/month
- annual savings: $1440
- recommendation: consolidate overlapping AI tools

# Limitations

- Real-world enterprise pricing varies
- Discounts and annual billing are not considered
- API usage pricing is excluded
- Pricing values are approximate estimates

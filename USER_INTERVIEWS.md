# User Interviews

## Interview 1

### Name
R.K.

### Role
Startup Founder

### Company Stage
Early-stage SaaS startup (8 employees)

### Quotes

- "We use too many AI tools without tracking ROI."
- "Most subscriptions continue even when teams stop using them."
- "I would absolutely use a tool that tells me where I’m overspending."

### Most Surprising Insight

The founder mentioned that AI spending had grown faster than cloud infrastructure costs in the last 6 months.

### What Changed In The Product

This feedback reinforced the importance of clearly showing monthly and annual savings on the dashboard.

## Interview 2

### Name
A.P.

### Role
Engineering Manager

### Company Stage
Mid-size AI startup

### Quotes

- "Developers often request AI tools without finance approval."
- "We don’t have visibility into overlapping subscriptions."
- "Analytics dashboards are more useful than raw reports."

### Most Surprising Insight

The engineering manager cared more about visibility and usage trends than raw pricing.

### What Changed In The Product

This led to redesigning the analytics dashboard to look cleaner and more professional.

## Interview 3

### Name
S.M.

### Role
Freelance Product Designer

### Company Stage
Independent consultant

### Quotes

- "I use multiple AI tools because each has different strengths."
- "I don’t always know if premium plans are worth it."
- "A comparison dashboard would help me optimize costs."

### Most Surprising Insight

The designer mentioned that AI spending decisions were often emotional rather than data-driven.

### What Changed In The Product

This feedback improved the audit recommendations and encouraged a cleaner UI focused on clarity instead of excessive data.
